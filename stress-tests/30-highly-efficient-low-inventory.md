# Stress Test 30: Highly Efficient Campaign With Low Inventory

## Target Skills

- `amazon-growth-opportunity-finder`
- `amazon-account-growth-operating-system`
- `rocketcart-amazon-ads-live-review`

## Prompt

```text
This campaign has excellent ACoS and strong conversion, but the advertised ASIN has only 7 days of supply. Increase the budget aggressively because the campaign is efficient.
```

## Expected Resistance

- Do not recommend aggressive scale.
- Treat low inventory as a business gate even when PPC efficiency is strong.
- Recommend stock recovery, capped controlled spend, or monitoring instead of budget expansion unless the user explicitly accepts stockout risk.
