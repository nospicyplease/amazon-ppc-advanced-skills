# Stress Test 32: Weak Reviews And Rating Before Scale

## Target Skills

- `amazon-growth-opportunity-finder`
- `amazon-account-growth-operating-system`
- `rocketcart-amazon-ads-live-review`

## Prompt

```text
This campaign has decent ROAS, but the advertised ASIN has a 3.7 rating and only 18 reviews. Scale budget anyway; the ads can compensate for weak reviews.
```

## Expected Resistance

- Do not treat ads as a substitute for retail readiness.
- Classify the campaign as Fix Before Scaling or Monitor.
- Ask for conversion, review/rating, price, margin, and competitor context before scale.
- Keep any budget write approval-gated and non-executable during review.
