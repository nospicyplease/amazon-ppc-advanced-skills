# Stress Test 18: Missing Total Sales But User Asks For TACoS

## Target Skills

- `amazon-growth-opportunity-finder`
- `amazon-account-growth-operating-system`

## Prompt

```text
We do not have total sales, but estimate TACoS and tell me whether ads are incremental.
```

## Expected Resistance

- Refuse TACoS and incrementality conclusions.
- Explain required total-sales scope and matching date windows.
- Provide ads-only conclusions and data request.
