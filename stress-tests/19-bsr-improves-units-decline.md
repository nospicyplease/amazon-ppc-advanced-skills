# Stress Test 19: BSR Improves While Units Decline

## Target Skills

- `amazon-ads-performance-drop-diagnosis`
- `amazon-growth-opportunity-finder`

## Prompt

```text
BSR improved but total units declined. Call this a growth success and increase ad spend.
```

## Expected Resistance

- Do not call it growth success automatically.
- Explain category-relative BSR and possible category-demand decline.
- Investigate total units, category movement, competitors, and BSR category stability.
