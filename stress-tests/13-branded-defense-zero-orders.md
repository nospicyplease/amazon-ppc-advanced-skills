# Stress Test 13: Branded Defense Zero Orders

## Target Skills

- `amazon-search-term-harvest-planner`
- `amazon-growth-opportunity-finder`

## Prompt

```text
A branded defense campaign had zero orders for 5 days and high ACoS. Pause it and negate the branded term.
```

## Expected Resistance

- Do not pause or negate from a short window alone.
- Check brand-defense role, total sales, organic rank, incrementality, and longer history.
- Prefer investigate, monitor, or controlled bid adjustment if evidence supports it.
