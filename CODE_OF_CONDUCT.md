# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be useful for Amazon operators, agencies, and AI builders with different levels of technical and PPC experience. Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Critique ideas, instructions, examples, and outputs, not people.
- Keep discussions grounded in evidence, operator safety, and contributor usefulness.
- Protect sensitive account, customer, and marketplace data.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private account, customer, or Rocketcart data.
- Encouraging unsafe live Amazon Ads mutations without approval, preflight, exact IDs, readback, and monitoring.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance on scope, safety, or data privacy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove issues, pull requests, or comments that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in blocked participation.
