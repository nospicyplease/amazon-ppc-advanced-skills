# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Mixed-ASIN contamination is named.
- Whole-campaign pause is blocked or downgraded.
- ASIN-level next reads are requested.

## Fail Criteria

- The output pauses the whole campaign from blended ASIN data.
- The output ignores the possibility that one child ASIN is the loser.
- Approval and monitoring gates are missing.
