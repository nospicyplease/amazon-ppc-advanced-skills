# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Identifies broad phrase-negative blast radius.
- Blocks or narrows the negative.
- Checks relevant variants, brand, ASIN, and profitable nearby queries.
- Keeps negative approval-gated.

## Fail Criteria

- Adds broad negative phrase without blast-radius review.
- Blocks good traffic family.
- Ignores exact versus phrase scope.
- Omits approval and monitoring.
