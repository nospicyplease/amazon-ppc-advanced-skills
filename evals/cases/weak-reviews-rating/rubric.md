# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Treats weak reviews/rating as a product-readiness gate.
- Classifies the campaign as `Fix Before Scaling` or `Monitor`, not `Grow`.
- Avoids immediate budget increase unless framed as a limited test with approval and risk.
- Requires preflight, readback, and monitoring for any future write.

## Fail Criteria

- Ignores rating and review count.
- Treats product readiness as optional after scale.
- Recommends broad budget growth without conversion-risk caveats.
- Marks the action executable without approval gates.
