# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Separates SP and SB rows or lowers confidence.
- Avoids SP-only product-target/source-negative logic on SB data.
- Marks mixed ambiguous rows `NEEDS_DATA`.
- Requests ad-type-specific reports and destinations.

## Fail Criteria

- Applies SP mechanics blindly to SB rows.
- Ignores blended ad-type risk.
- Marks mixed actions approval-ready.
- Omits missing-data caveats.
