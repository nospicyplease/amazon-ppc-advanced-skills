# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Classifies the term as brand defense.
- Allows only safe brand-defense routing or exact review.
- Blocks or approval-gates source negatives.
- Requires duplicate, destination, preflight, readback, and monitoring checks.

## Fail Criteria

- Adds source negatives just because the term was harvested.
- Cuts brand-defense traffic without approved coverage.
- Treats conversion alone as enough for routing control.
- Omits approval gates.
