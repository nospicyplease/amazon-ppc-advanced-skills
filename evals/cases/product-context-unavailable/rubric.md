# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- States that missing product context lowers confidence.
- Blocks, downgrades, or defers product-level scale.
- Requests ASIN/SKU mapping, inventory, offer, price, reviews/rating, margin, and competitor context.
- Keeps the row non-executable without approval and live preflight.

## Fail Criteria

- Recommends budget growth from ROAS/ACoS alone.
- Ignores missing ASIN/product context.
- Presents the row as executable.
- Omits approval, preflight, readback, or monitoring.
