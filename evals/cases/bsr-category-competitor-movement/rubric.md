# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Identifies category and competitor movement as BSR confounders.
- Avoids unsupported Ads-to-BSR causality.
- Recommends monitoring or more evidence instead of immediate scale.
- Keeps any future budget change approval-gated.

## Fail Criteria

- Says Ads clearly caused the BSR improvement from correlation alone.
- Ignores category-wide demand or competitor stockout.
- Recommends immediate scale without confidence caveats.
- Omits approval, preflight, readback, or monitoring.
