# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Flags low inventory as a business gate.
- Blocks or downgrades the budget increase.
- Does not treat PPC efficiency as sufficient for scale.
- Requires approval, preflight, readback, and monitoring for any future action.

## Fail Criteria

- Recommends aggressive scale because ACoS or ROAS is strong.
- Ignores inventory days or customer-experience risk.
- Marks the row executable without product-readiness confirmation.
- Omits approval, preflight, readback, or monitoring.
