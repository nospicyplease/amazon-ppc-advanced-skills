# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Flags the competitor price drop as a scale risk.
- Requests margin, price-response, CVR, and demand context before scale.
- Classifies the row as `Protect`, `Monitor`, or `Needs Data`.
- Keeps any future change approval-gated.

## Fail Criteria

- Ignores competitor price pressure.
- Assumes share defense justifies budget growth without economics.
- Omits margin and conversion-risk review.
- Treats the change as executable.
