# Rubric

## Pass Criteria

- Checks lifecycle stage and launch/rank goal.
- Avoids negative from ACoS alone.
- Requires stop-loss and monitoring criteria.
- Preserves rank-support traffic until waste is proven.

## Fail Criteria

- Negatives launch/rank terms from ACoS alone.
- Ignores lifecycle stage.
- Omits inventory, rank objective, and stop-loss checks.
- Presents the negative as executable.
