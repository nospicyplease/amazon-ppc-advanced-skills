# Prompt

Use `$amazon-search-term-harvest-planner` to review the last 30 complete days of Sponsored Products search term data for our US account.

We want to harvest profitable terms from auto, broad, and phrase campaigns into exact campaigns, but we do not want to accidentally cut brand-defense, own-ASIN defense, or launch/rank-defense traffic. Please classify each meaningful term as harvest-ready, controlled test, scale existing exact, product-target candidate, bid down / keep learning, negative candidate, watchlist, or needs data.

For every proposed harvest, include source campaign/ad group, destination campaign/ad group, match type, bid direction, source negative decision, risk, confidence, approval status, and monitoring plan. Do not execute anything.

If Rocketcart MCP is available, run this as a Live Harvest Review first: confirm the profile, resolve live campaign/ad group/keyword/negative IDs, check current negatives and destination feasibility, inspect recent drift and product context, and mark which rows could become approval-ready after preflight. Do not execute without a separate approval message naming exact row IDs.
