# Known Bad Output

```text
Ads caused the BSR drop. Immediately increase both exact campaign budgets and restore Top of Search. Pause the auto campaign because ACoS is high.
```

## Why This Fails

- Claims unsupported PPC-to-BSR causality.
- Executes or implies live budget/placement changes from static exports.
- Pauses a discovery campaign without search-term and strategic-role review.
- Omits missing competitor, bid-history, purchased-product, and coupon evidence.
- Omits preflight, exact current values, readback, and monitoring.
