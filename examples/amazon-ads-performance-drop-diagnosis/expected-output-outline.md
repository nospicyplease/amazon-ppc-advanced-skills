# Expected Output Outline

A good answer should include:

1. **Data Reliability And Actionability Gate**
   - Classify as `Actionable`, `Directional`, or `Non-actionable`.
   - State exact windows, ad type, ASIN scope, attribution caveats, missing competitor data, missing margin, and missing full bid history.
   - Explain whether bid/budget/placement recommendations are action-safe.

2. **Executive Verdict**
   - Identify the likely timing around April 30 to May 2.
   - Separate budget reduction, placement reduction, coupon expiry, and possible competitor factors.
   - Avoid claiming ads caused BSR deterioration unless total unit velocity and timing support the hypothesis.

3. **Drop Timeline**
   - Show when clicks, orders, total units, CVR, and BSR changed.
   - Include control changes within +/- 7 days.

4. **Impact Summary Table**
   - Compare baseline versus drop window using per-day values.
   - Include traffic, spend, conversion, BSR, and total-sales signals.

5. **Root-Cause Diagnosis**
   - Separate confirmed facts, likely causes, assumptions, and missing data.

6. **Biggest Losers**
   - Rank campaigns, placements, search terms, and ASINs by lost orders or sales per day.

7. **BSR And Competitor Interpretation**
   - Treat BSR as a velocity signal.
   - Flag missing competitor context as a confidence limiter.

8. **Recommended Actions**
   - Restore or test budget/placement only where history supports qualified traffic.
   - Do not recommend broad pauses or negatives from partial data.
   - Mark any live write as approval-gated with preflight and readback.

9. **Verification Plan**
   - Include 3-day, 7-day, and 14-day monitoring thresholds.
