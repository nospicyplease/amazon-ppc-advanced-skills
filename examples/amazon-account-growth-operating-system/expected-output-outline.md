# Expected Output Outline

A good answer should include:

1. **Executive Decision Summary**
   - State data reliability, growth posture, first action, biggest opportunity, biggest risk, and confidence.

2. **Single Prioritized Action Queue**
   - Include priority score, entity, action type, reason, expected impact, risk, confidence, timing, and approval requirement.
   - Protect urgent downside before scale.

3. **Protect List**
   - Include the high-revenue ASIN, lost Top of Search traffic, BSR risk, and budget/coupon context.

4. **Growth List**
   - Include budget-capped winners and harvestable terms only when evidence thresholds and readiness gates are met.

5. **Fix Before Scaling List**
   - Block aggressive scale for ASINs with low days of supply or missing margin.

6. **Budget Reallocation Plan**
   - Avoid exact amounts unless current budgets and risk tolerance support them.
   - Do not fund growth by cutting strategic defense or rank-supporting spend without evidence.

7. **Weekly Operating Plan**
   - Split actions into immediate, 3-day, 7-day, 14-day, and 30-day checks.

8. **Monitoring Rules**
   - Define success, failure, and next response for major actions.

9. **Human Approval Required**
   - Flag budget increases, major bid changes, negatives, or structural changes.

10. **Missing Data And Confidence Notes**
    - Name missing competitor, organic-rank, margin, and purchased-product data.
