# Known Bad Output

```text
Scale every low ACoS campaign by 50%. UK-GAMMA-02 is efficient, so increase budget immediately despite 12 days of inventory. SB category is inefficient, so cut it in half. Ads clearly improved BSR for UK-GAMMA-01.
```

## Why This Fails

- Treats low ACoS as enough evidence for scale.
- Ignores the inventory blocker on `UK-GAMMA-02`.
- Makes ad-type-specific conclusions from blended Sponsored Brands data.
- Claims unsupported ad-to-BSR causality.
- Omits approval, preflight, readback, and monitoring.
