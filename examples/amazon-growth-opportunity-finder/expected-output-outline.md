# Expected Output Outline

A good answer should include:

1. **Executive Summary**
   - Identify top growth opportunities, biggest blockers, and best budget stance.
   - State confidence and data limitations.

2. **Data Coverage And Trust**
   - List available reports, windows, ad-type coverage, missing competitor visibility, and Sponsored Brands caveats.

3. **Top Growth Opportunities Table**
   - Include ASIN/product, opportunity type, ads signal, BSR/organic signal, retail readiness, incrementality check, action, expected impact, risk, confidence, and priority score.

4. **Ads-Wise Findings**
   - Flag scale candidates, harvest candidates, bid-down candidates, budget-capped winners, placement opportunities, and waste.

5. **BSR-Wise Findings**
   - Treat BSR as supporting evidence, not proof.
   - Include category consistency, total units, TACoS, and organic-rank context.

6. **ASIN-Level Recommendations**
   - Check margin, inventory, Featured Offer / Buy Box, reviews, price, delivery promise, listing quality, conversion, and BSR before scale.

7. **Campaign And Keyword Actions**
   - Provide exact action rows and approval status.
   - Do not recommend negatives or pauses without sufficient waste evidence.

8. **Budget Reallocation Plan**
   - Move budget only from isolated waste or blocked growth to validated winners.

9. **Watchlist**
   - Use watchlist for low-sample terms, uncertain incrementality, or missing competitor context.

10. **Questions / Missing Data**
    - Prioritize the missing fields that would change actionability.
