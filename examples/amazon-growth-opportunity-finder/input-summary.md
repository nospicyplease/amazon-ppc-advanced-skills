# Input Summary

## Scope

- Marketplace: UK.
- Brand: anonymized kitchen accessories brand.
- Ad types: Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands available; Sponsored Products is most complete.
- Current window: last 30 complete days.
- Comparison window: prior 30 days.
- Goal: balanced profitable growth.

## Available Data

- Campaign, targeting, search term, placement, advertised-product, and purchased-product reports.
- Business Reports by child ASIN: sessions, unit session percentage, ordered units, and ordered revenue.
- Product economics: selling price, contribution margin percentage, target ACoS, and target CPA for top ASINs.
- Inventory and days of supply for all advertised ASINs.
- Featured Offer / Buy Box status from the latest retail-readiness pull.
- Review count, rating, price, coupon status, and delivery promise.
- BSR history by ASIN and category.
- Organic keyword rank for five priority category terms.

## Missing Data

- Competitor ad visibility.
- New-to-brand metrics for Sponsored Brands.
- Full attribution split between branded and non-branded for older campaigns.
- Detailed return-rate data.

## Assumptions

- Same-day data is excluded.
- Currency is GBP.
- Search terms with fewer than 2 orders are treated as low confidence unless strategically important.
- Inventory below 21 days of supply should block aggressive scale.
